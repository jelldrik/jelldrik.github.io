Privacy Policy

PubGolf App · Last updated: 14 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PubGolf App ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We have built the App to respect your privacy: there is no account, no login, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal contact information.

1. No Account Required

The App does not require you to register or sign in. Player names you enter during a game are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Anonymous Usage Analytics

To understand how the App is used and to improve it, we collect anonymous gameplay statistics. These are tied only to a randomly generated installation identifier (a random ID created on your device, stored locally), which is not linked to your identity and cannot be used to identify you personally. We collect:

  • Game session data: number of players, number of holes, total round duration, time spent per hole, scores, and whether the round was completed.
  • Feature usage: how a course was created (e.g. built manually, AI suggestions, nearby-bar suggestions, or an existing course) and whether the AI course feature was used.
  • The city and country of the first venue in a round (derived from the venue, not from your device location), and the App version.

This analytics data is stored on Google Firebase Realtime Database.

3. Optional Feedback

If you choose to submit an in-app rating (1–5 stars) and an optional written comment, we store this feedback together with the anonymous installation identifier so we can improve the App. Providing feedback is entirely optional.

4. Location

With your permission, the App accesses your device location to center the map on your position and to find pubs and bars near you (using Apple Maps search on your device). Your device location is processed on your device only and is never uploaded to our servers. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device Settings; the App remains usable.

5. Community Courses

If you choose to publish a pub golf course so other players can discover and play it, we store the information you provide for that course on our servers, including: the course name you choose, the venue names, drink and par for each hole, the venue coordinates, and basic metadata (number of holes, walking distance, App version). Venue coordinates are the locations of publicly searchable bars/pubs — not your personal location. Publishing a course is optional and only happens when you confirm it.

6. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads are non-personalized. The App does not ask permission to track you across other apps and websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for tracking. Google may process limited information required to serve and measure non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove ads entirely by purchasing the Premium subscription or the ad-free option.

7. Purchases

In-app purchases, subscriptions, and donations (tips) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. The App only stores locally on your device whether you have an active subscription or unlock, in order to enable the features you purchased.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database — stores anonymous analytics, feedback, and published community courses. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google AdMob — serves non-personalized ads. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — handles all purchases and standard App Store services.

9. Data Retention & Your Choices

Because we do not collect personal information and the analytics data is anonymous, we cannot identify the data belonging to a specific person. You can stop all analytics collection by deleting the App. If you have published a community course and would like it removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

10. Children

The App is intended for an adult audience (a game centered on visiting pubs and bars) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
